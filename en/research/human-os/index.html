What is Human OS?

Human OS is Eudaimonia Universe's proprietary framework for structuring how people perceive the world and act according to what they value.

Developed by: Eudaimonia Universe Author: Takayuki Mizuno (Founder & CEO) Page published: 2026-07-30 Last reviewed: 2026-09-07
Executive Summary

Overview

At its centre is the value map — the common source of what matters, from which every judgement follows. From there, two parallel functions operate side by side: the cognitive window (how the world appears to you) and competency (what you can bring to bear). These are not layered in a hierarchy; they work as equals, in parallel.

Two dimensions cut across both functions: bias (a way of bending) and stability (a way of possibly breaking) — tested when something you care about is at risk.

The organisational problem it addresses

The gap between how capable a person is and what they actually deliver, and the difficulty of explaining why the same person performs in one situation and not in another. Interventions miss when a remedy is chosen from a reading of ability alone, or of perception alone, without looking at what the person values.

Background & Method

Development background and method

Human OS was formalised from observation in organisational engagements and in the operation of our own products. A highly capable person with a high vantage point sometimes fails to perform consistently, while someone who was never visible before suddenly steps up in a crisis. To explain that swing, we placed the value map as the common source, set the cognitive window and competency alongside each other as parallel functions, and made bias and stability the dimensions that cut across both. The structure has been tested and revised through subsequent engagements and products. The theory is version-controlled in a single repository, and every product references the same definitions.

Evidence status

Human OS is a practical organising framework developed by Eudaimonia Universe through practice. It is currently being evaluated and is not presented as peer-reviewed, empirically validated research. It is not presented as a replacement for established psychological, organisational behaviour, or management theories.

Application

Organisational application

  • A shared vocabulary for diagnosis: survey and interview findings are organised as what is happening in the value map, the cognitive window, or competency, so that leadership and the front line can describe the current state in the same words.
  • Conflict diagnosis: combined with Conflictology, it separates a difference in how something is seen from a difference in what is valued, before an intervention is designed.
  • The theory layer of our products: our products, including Compass, reference Human OS as their shared theoretical base. The measurement of the cognitive window is the theoretical basis for Compass.

Limitations

Human OS is an organising framework for practice, not a measurement scale in itself. Its distinctions are diagnostic instruments; they do not reduce human complexity. How the structure expresses itself across cultures is still being examined.
