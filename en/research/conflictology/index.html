Conflictology

What is Conflictology?

Conflictology is Eudaimonia Universe's framework for diagnosing different sources of organisational conflict before selecting an intervention. It classifies conflict into four types: value, structure, cognition, and existence.

Conflict is diagnosable.

Name the type before you choose the remedy.

Developed by: Eudaimonia Universe Author: Takayuki Mizuno (Founder & CEO) Page published: 2026-07-30 Last reviewed: 2026-09-07
Executive Summary

Overview

Conflict arises when several things that are hard to reconcile — the conditions of the field, ways of seeing, competing goods, or ways of being — stand side by side, producing structural tension. We treat conflict not as a problem to be solved, but as a structural fact of the world, addressed without value judgement. Diagnosis determines the intervention.

A conflict has a type, and the type can be identified before any remedy is chosen. Choosing a remedy without naming the type is the most common reason dialogue fails. Diagnosis precedes prescription, as it does in medicine.

The organisational problem it addresses

The same conflict recurring between functions, cultural collision after an integration, executive meetings that never close. Conflict filed under "personality" always comes back: the remedies multiply because the cause was never named.

Structure

The four types

Diagnose the structure first

We diagnose the structure first — whether conditions such as power imbalance, dependency, or difficulty of exit still allow people to speak candidly. Working on the content while the field itself is broken rarely surfaces the truth.

Structure

Incentives, roles or processes place the parties in opposition regardless of intent. The question is whether the field allows candid speech at all.

Once the field holds, distinguish the content along three axes

Cognition is

A mismatch in how the same phenomenon appears. It dissolves once perspectives align.

Value ought

A clash between two legitimate goods. It remains even after perspectives align.

Existence who

A way of being that cannot move. Understanding alone doesn't resolve it; the person's standpoint has to be reconstructed.

Each axis calls for a different intervention, and using the wrong one for the words involved misses the mark. Sequence matters too: working through structure, then cognition and value, then existence, accounts for most of what makes an intervention work.

Background & Method

Development background and method

Conflictology was formalised from a recurring field observation: the same remedy works on one conflict and fails completely on another. We located the reason in the type of the conflict rather than in the quality of the remedy, proposed the four-type classification as a hypothesis, and have continued to test and revise it through engagements and through products such as Compass and Integration Compass.

Evidence status

Conflictology is a proprietary diagnostic framework developed by Eudaimonia Universe through practice. It is currently being evaluated and is not presented as peer-reviewed, empirically validated research. It is not presented as a replacement for established fields such as conflict resolution studies or negotiation theory; it is an organising framework for practical diagnosis.

Application

Organisational application

  • Diagnosing organisational conflict: cross-functional friction, executive discord, and post-merger cultural collision are diagnosed through the four types before an intervention is designed.
  • Decision design: the questions at issue in stalled meetings are structured by type, and the consent process is rebuilt.
  • Leadership development: reading the type of a conflict becomes a shared leadership skill (product: Compass).

Limitations

The four types are diagnostic instruments; real conflicts usually present as overlapping types. Classification is not itself an intervention: the centre of the work lies in the design and dialogue that follow. The boundaries of applicability are still being examined.

Questions

Frequently asked

What does "conflict is diagnosable" mean?
It means a conflict has a type, and the type can be identified before any remedy is chosen. We check first whether the field still allows candid speech (structure), then distinguish the content along three axes: cognition, value and existence. The internal structure of the people involved is read through Human OS. Diagnosis precedes prescription, as it does in medicine.
Why does dialogue alone fail to resolve organisational conflict?
Because conflict is a structural phenomenon with several causes acting at once. Without identifying which type is dominant, further conversation applies remedies that do not match the cause. Naming the type first is what makes the subsequent conversation productive.
What does intervention mean in practice?
A concrete move against the structure of a conflict. It may mean redesigning the question a team is arguing about, rebuilding the meeting or the consent process, or altering roles and the setting for dialogue. The move is selected from the diagnosis.
How does an engagement proceed?
In four stages: structure, diagnose, intervene, emergence. We read the situation through Human OS, classify the conflict through the four types of Conflictology, design an intervention matched to that classification, and work towards an answer neither side held at the outset.
