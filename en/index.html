Organizational Challenges

Organizational challenges
are also human challenges.

Strategy, structure, and systems are essential. Yet organizations do not change through systems alone.

Values, emotions, assumptions, meaning, and relationships shape how people make decisions, respond to change, and work together.

Signals we hear most often
  • Purpose that does not translate into everyday behavior
  • A widening gap between leadership and employees
  • Declining engagement and meaning at work
  • Repeated conflict between teams or functions
  • Change fatigue
  • Leadership teams struggling to make difficult decisions
  • Short-term performance at the expense of long-term organizational health

Questions

Frequently asked

What is Eudaimonia Universe?
Eudaimonia Universe is a human science research and organizational transformation company based in Japan. We turn original research on values, emotions, cognition, meaning, and relationships into practical frameworks, diagnostics, and technologies that help organizations strengthen culture, leadership, decision-making, and meaningful work. Founded and led by Takayuki Mizuno.
What services do you provide?
Three core services run with organizations: Culture Diagnostics, which quantifies organizational culture from audit logs alone; Okagesama, which brings noticing of mutual support into the tools teams already use; and Compass, which works through a real conflict structurally in dialogue with AI. They connect to six areas: organizational transformation, leadership and decision-making, engagement and meaningful work, organizational conflict, sustainable value creation, and business and innovation. Every engagement moves through five stages: understand, diagnose, design, transform, and embed. Diagnosis draws on our original frameworks, including Human OS and Conflictology. See What We Do.
What is Human OS?
Human OS is a model of a person's internal structure in five layers: Body, Emotion, Mind, Meaning and Relationship. In a diagnosis it functions as a shared vocabulary for identifying which layer a difficulty actually sits in, before anyone proposes a remedy.
What is Conflictology?
Conflictology is a framework that classifies conflict into four types: cognition, where facts are read differently; value, where priorities differ; structure, where incentives place parties in opposition; and existence, where standing or identity feels threatened.
How is AI used?
AI supports the analysis; people design the intervention. Combining our frameworks with AI analysis lets us read a structure faster and in more detail. In our products, a person's own words are never passed to their manager; only aggregates are, enforced at the database view.
How do I get in touch?
Use the contact form or write to info@eudaimonialab.org. We take enquiries about organizational transformation, joint research on Human OS and Conflictology, investment and partnership, and press or speaking requests, in English or Japanese.
